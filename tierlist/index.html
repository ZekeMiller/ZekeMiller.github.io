Tier List

Add items

Tray

Drag items onto the board, and drag them back here to unplace. Double-click an item to rename it. Select it and press Delete to remove it.

Tiers

Click a tier's name to rename it. Hover a row to recolor, move, or delete it. Items in deleted tiers go back to the tray.

Scale

Drop items anywhere on the line between the two ends, and how far apart they are counts. Click either end's label to change it, for example “Worst” to “Best” or “Cozy” to “Chaotic”. Crowded items stack up but still point to their exact spot.
